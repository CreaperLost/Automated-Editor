## 1. The licence verdict

**There is none.** No `LICENSE` file in the archive, no licence statement on the blog, no
"free to use" line anywhere.

Under this folder's rule that is the same verdict as **kitcut** (no licence) and **video-talkcraft**
(PolyForm Noncommercial, refused): **study the method; do not copy the text.** This one is the
opposite of Editor V1, which carries an explicit bespoke permission
([finding 25](25-the-kit-behind-the-video.md) §1). The difference between two artefacts from the
same company, one week apart in publication, is worth noting on its own: **the one they wrote for
the public has a licence; the one that is actually their working method does not.**

## 2. The sound library is not in the download

The blog says *"Get the editing skill and sound library below to follow along"* and then embeds
exactly one asset: the skill zip — 11 markdown and yaml files, **no sounds**.

**This corrects the earlier wave.** [`research/competitors.md`](../research/competitors.md)
records the Higgsfield motion story as *"Its Editing Skill + SFX Library is sold as one download,
the clearest signal that taste and assets travel together."* The public download is the **skill
alone**. Either the sound library is delivered another way (the MCP, the app) or the promise and
the artefact do not match — but **what is published is a skill and no assets**, which weakens the
"taste and assets travel together" reading for this particular reference.

> cutting, screencasts and assets, short briefs over the frame, motion and checking the result.
> Apply to future tutorial episodes, **especially Higgsfield's**, and to analysing the author's

Four things follow, and they are the reason this artefact is worth reading:

2. **The reference is the author's own final cut.** Everything measurable was measured against

4. **It names the skill it must not use.** *"не подключай `youtube-visual-direction`, от которого Макс

And one rule that is worth more than the whole rest of the file:

## 4. The four-second rule, again — from a completely different direction

The camera reference:

> | Задача | Ракурс | Момент смены |
> |---|---|---|
> | Основное обращение, вывод, короткая надпись | A | Законченная фраза/жест, завершённый акцент |
> | Два небольших визуала | A, проверенные свободные зоны | Когда связь объектов прочитана |
> | Большой ассет, последовательность карточек, схема | C, в проверенной свободной части кадра | Когда объяснение завершено или визуал ушёл |
> | Реакция, обещание показа, связка, новый кейс | B без наложений | Конец самостоятельной реплики, перед демонстрацией |
> | Несовпадающая поза на речевом стыке | Другая синхронная камера | Безопасное место между словами |
> | Действие в программе | Крупный скринкаст, при необходимости маленькая A | После действия и читаемого результата |

> "В чистой речи ориентир — **около четырёх секунд.** Ищи естественный словесный и пластический
> стык. **Не делай механическую ротацию A–B–C.**"
>
> *[In clean speech the reference point is **about four seconds.** Look for a natural verbal and
> physical joint. **Do not do a mechanical A–B–C rotation.**]*

**This is the second independent four-second artefact in this folder.** Editor V1 derived *"a
4-second hold is a mistake"* by measuring three documentary reels (held-shot median 0.56–0.79 s,
p90 1.10–1.77 s). Max arrived at *"около четырёх секунд"* for his own tutorial videos, on a
different corpus, in a different language, for a different format.

Two unrelated derivations landing on the same number is the strongest evidence in this folder that
**~4 s is the field's real ceiling for "how long one visual holds before a change is due"** — not
Editor V1's taste and not Max's. It is worth a rule.

**The A/B/C model is also finding [01](01-treatment-model-and-visual-lead.md)'s three-treatment
idea, one level up.** There the decision is *graphic, speaker or full-frame*; here it is *which
camera, and what may go on it* — with the extra rule that **a visible B carries no graphics at
all**, and that the free space is checked in the current material (*"проверь свободное место рядом с
ведущим в текущем материале: оно может быть слева или справа"* — the free space beside the
speaker may be left or right; **don't inherit it from the last project**).

And the measured table — **Max's own edit against the author's final**:

| Camera | Median of Max's version | Median of the author's final | Holds in the final |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 3,98 s | 5,12 s | 52 |
| B | 3,92 s | 3,58 s | 13 |
| C | 7,12 s | 7,02 s | 44 |

**A concrete, measured correction: Max's edit was cutting A too fast** (3.98 s against 5.12 s) —
and he says so. Then, immediately: *"Не превращай медианы и количества в норматив следующего
выпуска"* — **don't turn the medians and counts into a norm for the next episode.**

## 5. "Blinking" — the defect class with a name

This is the most valuable single idea in the artefact, and it is the one our gate is blindest to.

> ## Непрерывность визуалов без «моргания»
>
> Считай длительность **видимого кадра.** Длинная камера на нижней дорожке может открываться всего
> на несколько кадров между двумя верхними вставками. **Это такой же короткий случайный шот, как
> отдельный отрезок камеры.**
>
> *[Measure the duration of the **visible frame.** A long camera on the lower track may open for
> just a few frames between two upper inserts. **That is the same short accidental shot as a
> separate camera cut.**]*

The named defect is **"blinking"** (*моргание*): the speaker, or another angle, appearing for a few
frames between two inserts. And the rules around it are precise:

- **Coincident clip edges do not guarantee continuity.** A fade-out, a transparent first frame, a
  mask or a too-early background exit can open the lower camera. *"Проверяй всю композицию на входе
  и выходе, включая фон и подписи"* — check the whole composition on entry and exit, **including the
  background and the captions**.
- The worked example: `screencast → 9 frames of C → A on the line` is fixed by a direct cut
  `screencast → A`, keeping A's sync.
- *"Короткие видимые фрагменты, особенно меньше секунды, — **кандидаты на просмотр, а не автоматическое
  правило удаления.**"* — sub-second visible fragments are **candidates for review, not an automatic
  removal rule.**
- And from the Passport Rush table: *"Между визуалами на мгновение открывалась ведущая; **длинный
  нижний клип маскировал проблему в списке длительностей**"* — **a long lower clip masked the
  problem in the duration list.**

**That last clause is our blind spot, named exactly.** Our gate measures a graphic in isolation; our
storyboard lists scenes. **A duration list cannot see a layer problem** — because the duration is
the clip's, not the visible frame's. The fix is to compute **visible intervals**: for each lower
layer, the spans during which every upper layer is opaque. We have the layer model, the track
model and the graphic's opacity timeline. **We have never computed a visible interval.**

One related rule from the same file: *"Соседние клипы одного источника с непрерывным исходным
временем могут составлять один непрерывный кадр. Не считай их jump cut только из-за двух клипов на
таймлайне"* — adjacent clips from the same source with continuous source time may be **one**
continuous shot; don't call it a jump cut just because there are two clips on the timeline. Our cut
detection should not treat the timeline's clip boundaries as the truth.

## 6. The learning record — the best AGT-3 artefact in the folder

`references/learned-examples.md` is exactly what our AGT-3 wants: **what was learned from the
user's manual edits, with the evidence attached.**

Fifteen confirmed changes, each with a timestamp, what changed in the author's final, and a
**repeatable conclusion**:

> | Место | Что изменено в авторском финале | Повторяемый вывод |
> |---|---|---|
> | 00:20–00:25 | Плоский список заменён последовательными DIRECTION / BLOCKING / FINAL VIDEO с перспективой на C | Раскрывать по смысловым словам и вписывать в свободное пространство |
> | 03:57–04:00 | Нужный промпт крупнее, окружение приглушено | Один точный читаемый объект внимания вместо общего мелкого UI |
> | 07:33–07:45 | Этапы раскрываются короткими группами, затем заменяются цепочкой BLOCKING → SEEDANCE 2.5 → FINISHED SHOT | Не выводить весь список одновременно; синхронизировать смены по речи |
> | 14:20–14:41 | Использован очищенный от служебной плашки чат | Cleanup отвлекающего элемента отделять от исправления фактов |

Plus **27 Rich Text briefs** extracted from the final, described as *"готовые слова, точный
источник, конкретная доработка и привязанный к кадру интервал"* — final words, an exact source, a
specific adjustment, and an interval bound to a frame.

And then the part that makes it trustworthy — **"Ограничения обобщения"**, the limits of
generalisation:

> - В финале было 12–13 цветовых нод с Higgs YT V3 против одной в моей версии. … **Число нод, точные
>   неизвестные настройки и названия версий не являются рецептом.**
> - Белый/лаймовый текст, тёмные тёплые фоны и перспектива — **наблюдаемый стиль этого выпуска, не
>   требование для любого бренда.**
> - В одной графике показаны 72.3% / 27.7%. **Их подлинность не подтверждена анализом монтажа. Не
>   переносить эти числа или чужие analytics в новый выпуск без его данных.**
> - Точные шрифты и кривые новых готовых MOV не восстановлены; можно воспроизводить наблюдаемую
>   подачу, но **нельзя утверждать неизвестные параметры.**

**Every one of those is a rule about what may not be learned.** And the epistemic rule is the
sharpest line in the whole artefact:

> "Сохраняй различие между **словами пользователя, наблюдением и собственной гипотезой.** Новые
> подтверждённые правки уточняют процесс; **один необычный кадр не должен создавать универсальное
> правило.**"
>
> *[Keep the distinction between **the user's words, an observation, and your own hypothesis.** New
> confirmed edits refine the process; **one unusual frame must not create a universal rule.**]*

**Three tiers of epistemic status** — what the user said, what was seen, what was inferred — is
more precise than anything our `gaps.md` and `decisions.md` currently express, and it is exactly
what a checkpoint-diff learning loop needs: our diffs currently distinguish *before* from *after*,
not *said* from *observed* from *hypothesised*.

And the artefact records **its own verification gap**:

> "Первичный разбор включал … полное техническое декодирование. **Он не включал непрерывное
> прослушивание всех 15 минут. Будущая финальная проверка должна явно учитывать этот пробел, а не
> наследовать заявление «всё просмотрено».**"
>
> *[The initial analysis included … a full technical decode. **It did not include continuous
> listening of all 15 minutes. A future final check must explicitly account for that gap, rather
> than inheriting the claim "everything was watched".**]*

**A skill that refuses to let its own completeness claim be inherited.** That is the discipline
finding [24](24-astra-the-vendors-own-numbers.md) saw from the outside — *"Hear that pop?
Terrible"* — written from the inside as a rule.

## 7. The checks — staged, and honest about what a check proves

`references/quality.md` is organised **by the commissioned stage**, not by a single list:

> "Не заставляй этап рыбы проходить все операции финального мастера. Но каждую фактическую правку
> доводи до соответствующей проверки."
>
> *[Don't force the "fish" stage through all the final-master operations. But take every actual edit
> to its corresponding check.]*

That is our per-preset steps, done properly: a stage has its own checks, and the set of checks
depends on what was actually done.

And the honesty rules are the part to steal:

> **Покадровые метрики помогают найти чёрные кадры, вспышки и остановки, но кандидат не является
> автоматически ошибкой:** неподвижный промпт или аутро могут быть намеренными.
>
> *[Per-frame metrics help find black frames, flashes and stalls, **but a candidate is not
> automatically an error:** a static prompt or an outro may be intentional.]*

> **Просмотри всю финальную программу в движении со звуком** … **Выборочные кадры и полное
> техническое декодирование не заменяют этот просмотр.** Если текущие инструменты не дают полного
> воспроизведения/прослушивания, укажи это и **не заявляй, что проверка полностью выполнена.**
>
> *[Watch the whole final programme in motion with sound … **Sampled frames and a full technical
> decode do not replace this viewing.** If the tools cannot play it through, say so and **do not
> claim the check was fully done.**]*

> **Автоматический список коротких интервалов служит для поиска, а не доказывает отсутствие ошибок.**
>
> *[An automatic list of short intervals serves to find things, **not to prove their absence.**]*

> Не обещай отсутствие всех ошибок на основании одних числовых метрик.
>
> *[Don't promise the absence of all errors on the basis of numeric metrics alone.]*

**"A candidate is not automatically an error"** is finding [07](07-motion-taste-checklist.md)'s
severity tiering and finding [17](17-what-the-seven-agree-on.md)'s "the check must read the output",
both arriving at once — plus the reporting discipline that a check must say **what it covered**.

And one audio rule that is directly checkable on our side:

> **Sample peak, RMS, true peak и LUFS — разные величины; не выдавай одно за другое.**
>
> *[Sample peak, RMS, true peak and LUFS are different quantities; **don't pass one off as
> another.**]*

We implement K-weighted `integrated_lufs` and we also measure peaks. **Do not report one as the
other.**

## 8. The brief grammar

> Формула: **что показать → где/какая камера → на каких словах появляется и меняется → когда уходит.**
>
> *[The formula: **what to show → where / which camera → on which words it appears and changes → when
> it leaves.**]*

Four slots, and a note that they must be on *separate editable text layers* in the project, **off
in the clean export but kept in the project** — so the brief is an artefact in the edit, not a
document beside it.

The lesson from the 27 extracted briefs is about *how* to write one:

> Мои прежние ТЗ перегружались историей поиска, техническими оговорками и повторяющимися запретами. Не
> пересказывай их поверх кадра. **Вместо «сделать красиво» опиши видимый результат; вместо общего
> «highlight prompt» назови строку; вместо общего «по речи» для сложного перечисления укажи
> слова-триггеры.**
>
> *[My previous briefs were overloaded with search history, technical caveats and repeated
> prohibitions. Don't retell them over the frame. **Instead of "make it beautiful", describe the
> visible result; instead of a general "highlight prompt", name the line; instead of a general "on
> speech", name the trigger words.**]*

**And the gap convention**, which is a rule about honesty in the edit itself:

> **НУЖЕН SC:** установка Blender-плагина. … До получения записи оставить это ТЗ. Заменяй такой
> пробел схемой только при согласованном решении; **схема объясняет последовательность, а не
> подтверждает реальное выполнение.**
>
> *[NEED SC: Blender plugin install. … Leave this brief until the recording arrives. Replace such a
> gap with a diagram only by agreement; **a diagram explains the sequence, it does not confirm real
> execution.**]*

The same rule appears in `visuals.md`: *"Контактный лист помогает навигации, **но не доказывает, что
действие произошло между выбранными кадрами**"* — a contact sheet helps navigation but does not
prove the action happened between the chosen frames.

**That is a direct objection to our `storyboard`.** Our storyboard shows tiles of the finished edit
with what is said; it is a navigation aid, and treating it as evidence would be exactly this
mistake. The parallel rule in `visuals.md` — *"если на выходе человек ещё дописывает нужную фразу,
ускорь более ранний ввод/ожидание или продли вставку"* — is the same idea for the edit itself: if
the action is not finished when the insert ends, the insert is wrong.

## 9. Resolve without losing editability — the strongest case for our own decision

`references/resolve.md` is titled *"Работа в Resolve без потери редактируемости"* — **Working in
Resolve without losing editability** — and it is a catalogue of ways a host's automation
*silently* breaks the edit:

> - … **после операции считывай фактическое состояние, а не только возвращённый успех.**
>   *[after an operation, read back the actual state, not just the returned success.]*
> - **Успех `ImportFusionComp` ещё не доказывает правильный выход.** … **`SetInput` мог возвращать
>   None при успешной записи — читай значение обратно.**
>   *[A successful `ImportFusionComp` does not prove the right output. … `SetInput` could return
>   None while actually writing — read the value back.]*
> - **Экспорт `.comp` мог сериализовать MediaIn/MediaOut как Loader/Saver.**
>   *[A `.comp` export could serialise MediaIn/MediaOut as Loader/Saver.]*
> - **Не принимай такую копию только по успешному ответу API.** … В Passport Rush пробная копия
>   скринкаста показывала более раннюю стадию рисования, хотя имя и длительность выглядели верно.
>   *[Don't accept such a copy on the strength of a successful API response. … In Passport Rush a
>   test copy of the screencast showed an earlier stage of the drawing, though its name and
>   duration looked right.]*
> - Для UI используй доступный способ computer use с **актуальными** снимками/состоянием.
>   **Не кликай по координатам старого скриншота.**
>   *[For the UI, use computer use with **current** screenshots. **Don't click at coordinates from an
>   old screenshot.**]*
> - **Неудавшиеся два ExportLUT означают отсутствие сравнения, а не совпадение LUT.**
>   *[Two failed ExportLUTs means there is no comparison, not that the LUTs match.]*

**Every one of those is an argument for a typed API over UI-driving** — and, more precisely, an
argument for *verifying state after every write*, which is a discipline we have (our gate reads the
render) but do not apply to the edit document.

But note the split they land on, because it is *ours*:

> "Предпочитай доступный API для измеримых операций; UI используй для недоступных через API действий
> и просмотра результата."
>
> *[Prefer the available API for measurable operations; use the UI for actions not available through
> the API and for viewing the result.]*

**API for measurable operations, UI for what the API cannot do and for looking at the outcome.** Our
`decisions.md` refuses driving an NLE by computer use because *"our typed API owns edits"*. This
artefact, from the vendor that *does* drive Resolve by computer use, arrives at the same boundary
from the other side — and adds the warning that the UI path requires *current* screenshots, never
coordinates from a stale one.

## 10. Two opposite architectures, one discipline

| | Editor V1 ([25](25-the-kit-behind-the-video.md), [26](26-the-style-is-a-program.md)) | this skill |
|---|---|---|
| artefact | 79 files, code, a renderer | 11 files, prose, no code |
| host | HyperFrames — a renderer we already ship | DaVinci Resolve, driven by computer use |
| the look | tokens + a seeded treatment engine | *"Мягкий естественный цвет"*, a remote grade named V3 |
| the reference | three documentary reels, measured frame by frame | one episode, measured against the author's final |
| the numbers | median holds, changes/min, LU under voice | A/B/C medians, 4 s, node counts, 15-minute gap |
| the learning | *"What is not measured (decisions)"* | *"Ограничения обобщения"* + three epistemic tiers |
| the check | lint → snapshot → look → render → re-transcribe | staged checks → watch the whole programme in motion |
| the licence | explicit bespoke permission | **none** |

**Same discipline, opposite implementations.** One writes a program that renders; the other writes
prose that drives a commercial NLE. Both arrive at the same three things: **measure the reference;
write down what was measured and what was chosen; say honestly what the check covered.**

That is the transferable part, and it is not the code.

## 11. What we would take

1. **Visible intervals.** Compute, for every lower layer, the spans during which all upper layers
   are opaque. A four-frame hole where the speaker appears is invisible in a duration list — the
   artefact says so explicitly — and our gate, our storyboard and our cut list are all duration
   based. **"Blinking" is a name for a defect class we cannot currently see.**
2. **The four-second hold, now corroborated twice.** Editor V1 derived it from three reels; Max
   arrived at it independently from his own episodes. It is the field's number, not one person's
   taste.
3. **Three tiers of epistemic status** — the user's words, an observation, our own hypothesis — as
   the output format of the checkpoint-diff learning loop, instead of a bare before/after diff.
4. **A "limits of generalisation" section** in anything we learn from the user, so a learned rule
   says what it may not be applied to.
5. **Record our own verification gaps** and refuse to inherit a completeness claim we did not earn.
   This is the strongest single idea in the artefact.
6. **Candidates are not errors.** Our gate's findings should say "candidate" where the rule is
   about taste, and a sub-second visible fragment should be routed to a review, not to a removal.
7. **A brief grammar with four slots** — what, where/camera, which words, when it leaves — as the
   format for our graphics briefs, so the agent's output is a thing that can be executed on a frame.
8. **"A diagram explains the sequence, it does not confirm real execution."** Our storyboard is a
   navigation aid; we must not let it be read as evidence.
9. **Read back state after every write.** Their `SetInput` returned None while writing; their API
   import silently dropped the media link. Our gate reads the render, but the *edit document*
   should also be read back after a pass, not assumed.
10. **Don't pass one loudness measure off as another** — sample peak, RMS, true peak and LUFS are
    four different quantities, and we implement K-weighted LUFS.
11. **Adjacent clips with continuous source time may be one shot** — our cut detection should not
    treat the timeline's clip boundaries as the truth.

## Caveats

- **The artefact was read, not run.** No Resolve project was opened, no automation was attempted,
  and none of its rules was tested against an edit.
- **It is Russian**, and the translations in brackets are mine. Where a rule's precision matters,
  the verbatim Russian is in the evidence capture — treat that as the source.
- **It is one editor's process for one show**, on a single sample episode ("Blender #2"), with a
  second episode ("Passport Rush") as a partial second sample. The A/B/C medians come from one
  final cut; they are not a population.
- **The four-second number is a reference point, not a rule**: *"около четырёх секунд"*, with the
  explicit instruction to look for a natural joint rather than to rotate mechanically.
- **No licence.** The archive carries none, and the blog does not state one. **Study it; do not
  copy the text.**
- **The sound library is not in the download**, so this artefact says nothing about how Higgsfield
  ships its sounds — only that the promised bundle does not contain them.
- **The A/B/C model is not portable as-is.** It assumes three synchronised cameras and a specific
  studio setup, which is a different shape from a screen recording.
