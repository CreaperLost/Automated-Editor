## reference/reference-log.md — verbatim (the derivation record)

> # Editor V1 — reference log (method and raw numbers)
>
> The raw analysis data (downloads, every frame, stems, per-frame JSON, contact sheets) is not
> shipped: it is frames and audio of other people's videos. Only the measurements are written
> down here and in `edl/`.
>
> ## The references
> Three documentary-style short-form reels, faceless voice-over over real footage and images,
> each ending on a "link in bio" card and the channel's mark.
> | id | what | size | fps | dur |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | REF-1 | a founders story ("the mafia" of one company) | 1916×1078 (also a 1280×720 copy, same cut) | 24 | 78.4s |
> | REF-2 | a game-theory explainer (signaling / burning the boats / information cascade) | 1440×1080 (also 960×720) | 25 | 93.6s |
> | REF-3 | a company-origin story (cockroach startups / the cereal boxes) | 1916×1078 | 25 | 70.5s |
>
> The method behind the skill: references → frame-by-frame analysis → a named style in a
> library of reusable devices → Claude sources footage for every line.
>
> ## Method
> 1. Per frame: 96×54 grey diff to the previous frame, HSV-histogram Bhattacharyya distance,
>    luma/saturation, colourfulness, hue histogram of saturated px.
> 2. A cut where the diff > max(14, 4× the local median) or hist distance > 0.5.
> 3. Contact sheets at 2fps over the whole runtime, full-frame-rate strips across every
>    distinct transition, full-res stills for type, grid and texture.
> 4. `demucs --two-stems=vocals` → levels, spectral-flux onsets, onset↔cut sync, periodicity,
>    and log-frequency spectrograms with cut markers.
> 5. Caption sync: count near-white px in the centre band per frame; jumps vs ASR word onsets.
>
> ## Numbers
> **Cutting**
> | | REF-1 | REF-2 | REF-3 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | detected changes / min | 81 | 164 | 175 |
> | held shots (≥0.25s) median | 0.79s | 0.56s | — |
> | held shots p90 | 1.77s | 1.10s | — |
> | strobe/flash (<0.17s) share of runtime | 3.7% | 13.2% | high |
>
> **Look (share of held runtime)**
> | | REF-1 | REF-2 |
> |---|---|---|
> | natural colour | 42% | 32% |
> | teal/cyan cast | 22% | 7% |
> | white/paper ground | 13% | 11% |
> | warm/orange | 11% | 8% |
> | B&W | 6% | 18% |
> | black ground | 6% | 25% |
> | vignette edge/centre luma (median) | 0.86 | 0.59 |
> Hue mass of saturated pixels piles at 0–30° and 180–220° with almost nothing between:
> teal-and-orange. REF-1 carries a halftone dot screen in the shadows and a thirds grid.
>
> **Type** (720p measurements → 1080)
> - caption: asc–desc extent 33px on 720 (4.6% H) → Inter 600 48px on REF-2's 4:3 frame;
>   REF-1 at 16:9 reads ~62px (the token value). Centre 49–52% H, 51–53% W; words appear in
>   pre-laid slots with wide gaps.
> - key word: 71px x-height+ascender on 720 → ~150px at 1080.
> - red shock word: #EC020B / #F60104 / #F30105 → token #F2030A.
> - Caption onset vs spoken onset: "This" 0.16→0.125, "is" 0.40→0.417, "most" 0.80→0.792,
>   "powerful" 0.96→1.0, "with" 82.04→82.0, "product" 82.44→82.48, "about" 83.40→83.48 —
>   **±1 frame**.
>
> **Sound** (demucs split)
> | | REF-1 | REF-2 | REF-3 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | bed under voice (during speech) | 3.0 dB | 13.5 dB | 0.8 dB |
> | bed below −45 dB | 3% | 40% | 1% |
> | SFX/music onsets / min | 84 | 78 | 113 |
> | onsets within 2 frames of a cut | 24% | 46% | 69% |
> | strongest periodicity | 62 BPM | 115 BPM | 125 BPM |
> Click trains: REF-1 1.77–2.24s 13 clicks at 0.040s (1/frame during a tile reveal);
> REF-2 0.09–1.27s 16 clicks at 0.078s (1 per pop-in). REF-1's bed is tonal (piano/strings
> lines in the spectrogram) on a slow pulse; REF-2 opens on a sub drone and click trains,
> then staccato hits with silence between, starting on the ident at 8.6s.
>
> ## What is not measured (decisions)
> - The speaker on camera ~25–35% of runtime (the refs have no narrator on screen).
> - The speaker's camera RAW — no grade, no B&W, no LUT, no vignette; the matte RGB from the
>   raw plate too.
> - Bed ~5.5 LU under the voice, cut (not ducked) on structure — between the references'
>   0.8–3 dB and a quieter talking-head mix.
> - 30fps output; frame counts converted.

## reference/look.md — verbatim

> # Editor V1 — look, type, rhythm, layouts
>
> ## 1 · Treatments (`scripts/ev1_treat.py`)
> | look | when | measured share |
> |---|---|---|
> | `teal` split (teal shadows, cream highlights, 40% toward grey) | places, buildings, establishing | REF-1 22% |
> | `mono` hard B&W | documents, pricing, archival, a serious beat | REF-2 18% |
> | `warm` amber/sepia | fire, money, history, war | 8–11% |
> | `invert` negative | 1–2 frame flashes; a strobe image | — |
> | `natural` + push + vignette | footage that is already good, the hero world | 32–42% |
> | `recolor FROM-TO:TARGET` | when the speaker names a colour/variant of one thing | e.g. one aurora photo recoloured red / green / blue |
> | `halftone` (0.16–0.35) | stills that would look like stock otherwise | REF-1 throughout |
> `recolor` recolours fully then blends in RGB by the hue-band weight — rotating hues by the
> weight paints rainbow fringes (it did, first try).
>
> **Your face: NOT graded.** Your camera stays RAW on every face beat: no grade, no B&W, no LUT,
> no vignette — the found material carries the colour. The matte RGB for the cut-out comes from
> the raw plate too (`scripts/matte.py`). Re-time the camera only (`fps=30`, `-g 30`, no
> filters). No grain and no lifted blacks on anything.
>
> ## 2 · Type
> Three display tiers + one caption tier; never anything in between.
> - caption 62px Inter 600 white (REF-1 at 16:9; REF-2's 4:3 frame reads 48px), soft dark
>   shadow + faint bloom, centre of frame; over a rebuilt UI drop it to 86% so it never covers
>   the evidence; on a light page it turns ink (`captions_switch`, `components/ev1_more.py`).
> - key 150px Inter 700 · title 136px Playfair italic chrome · shock 176px Bodoni red caps.
> - **mega** — the huge red word (Inter 900, `--ev1-red`, ~560px) set BEHIND the speaker's head,
>   between the plate and the RVM matte, riding the same punch-in. The default treatment for
>   the stressed word on a face beat.
> - Words appear on their onsets in final slots; a phrase clears at the cut.
>
> ## 3 · Rhythm
> Reference pace: held shot median 0.56–0.79s, strobe bursts on single words, a ground
> change (black / paper / image / face) every few beats. For long-form: cold open and chapter
> opens at reference pace, body at 1.2–2.0s per picture, your face 25–35% of runtime in
> 1.2–2.5s stretches, never two face beats in a row without material between.
>
> ## 4 · Layouts — your face
> The references are faceless. A speaker on camera appears three ways:
> 1. **Face beat** (raw camera) — gestures, numbers on fingers, the conversational lines.
> 2. **Stressed face beat** (raw camera + the mega word behind the head via the matte) — the
>    caveat, the serious line.
> 3. **Cut-out** — the hero device (`cutout_world`): matted, standing in front of a world that
>    changes. One per chapter at most.
> The 25–35% face share is a decision, not a measurement.

## SKILL.md — verbatim extracts

> **Where the look comes from.** Three documentary-style short-form reels (REF-1 a founders
> story, 78.4 s; REF-2 a game-theory explainer, 93.6 s; REF-3 a company-origin story, 70.5 s)
> were taken apart frame by frame; the voice was split from the music and effects and the sound
> measured on its own. `reference/reference-log.md` has the method and every number,
> `reference/edl/REF-1..3.md` the shot-by-shot breakdowns, `reference/edl/SOUND.md` the sound.
> The references are faceless voice-over; this kit adds the speaker on camera (~25–35% of
> runtime) and the cut-out hero device. That share is a choice, not a measurement.

> ### ⭐ The eight things a first draft gets wrong
>
> A first draft built from stills, screenshots and synthetic ticks looked nothing like the
> references. The frame-by-frame breakdowns are the spec; where `devices.md`'s first pass and
> the `edl/` files disagree, the `edl/` files win.
>
> 1. **Moving footage, not stills.** 75% (REF-1), 62% (REF-2), 50% (REF-3) of runtime is moving
>    clips.
> 2. **Metaphors and puns on most lines.** 15% (REF-1) / 59% by time (REF-2) / 31% (REF-3).
>    "new models" → 1950s motor-show cars; "leaked" → a dripping tap; "nothing" → a tumbleweed;
>    "slap captions on" → a label-slapping machine. Names, companies and claims are always the
>    real thing.
> 3. **Everything moves:** a steady 4–7.5%/s zoom on 62/79 shots (`broll(rate=…)`), new cuts open
>    defocused and sharpen over ~9 frames (`focus_in=9`), hard punch-ins on the stressed word
>    (`punches=`), windows that grow in hard steps 40% → 63% → full.
> 4. **Pace:** ~60 (REF-1) to ~180 (REF-3) images a minute. A 4-second hold is a mistake.
> 5. **Strobes are shape-matched**: every image in a burst shares one shape (all circles, all
>    banknotes, all blades), 2–4 frames each, one click each. Random page crops are not a strobe.
> 6. **Captions change character:** 62 px base, italic serif for a stressed phrase, 2.3× bold for
>    the key word, ink on light pages (`captions(..., mode)`, `captions_switch`).
> 7. **Sound on every change, at the references' levels:** clicks only 2–7 dB under the voice,
>    landing 4–14 ms after the picture. The devices write the cues; `sfx/ev1kit` is synthesised to
>    the measured length, transient and level of each reference sound. No booms on flashes, no
>    whoosh on the wall, no typing sound.
> 8. **The bed carries the cuts.** It runs from frame 0 ~5.5 LU under the voice, is NOT ducked,
>    CUTS OUT under the punchline (it plays dry) and slams back on the next line, pulls down under
>    black/UI cards. Never put a drop or an impact ON a name: it masks the consonant ("Claude did."
>    re-transcribed as "Flaw dead"). Re-transcribe the final to prove no word got masked.

> ### ⛔ Hard rules
>
> 1. **Real material on every beat.** …
> 2. **Every found image is treated** (`scripts/ev1_treat.py`): 58–68% of held reference shots
>    carry a grade, the rest are at least pushed and vignetted. Never a raw screenshot edge to edge.
>    **The speaker's camera is the exception: RAW on every face beat** — no grade, no B&W, no LUT,
>    no vignette. The found material carries the colour. The cut-out's RGB comes from the raw plate.
> 3. **Every word on screen was said.** Captions are the speaker's words at their onsets, ±1 frame.
>    Titles are the thing named (a real name, a real number), never a slogan.
> 4. **Verify every fact while sourcing.** Numbers and claims from the real page, quoted exactly,
>    dated. Where two first-party pages disagree, say which one you used.
> …
> 8. **Rights are flagged before the render.** Every source goes in PLAN.md's table with its
>    licence; anything unclear (a company's film, a news photo, someone's clip) is marked FLAG and
>    the person decides. Bring your own music.
> 9. **Nothing publishes.** Render, mix, open `out/final.mp4` for review, stop. Never upload or post.

> ## 5 · Workflow
> …
> 2. **Plan and source** — `reference/sourcing.md`. For every line: the real thing (company page →
>    `scripts/capture.mjs`; company photo or archival → `scripts/find-footage.py`; the company's own
>    film → a short official clip; space/science → NASA; numbers → the real page), plus a metaphor
>    wherever the line allows. Write the beat table and the sources table (with licences and FLAGs)
>    into `PLAN.md`. **Show the person the plan and the flags, and wait for a go** before building.
> …
> 5. **Check:** `hf lint .` (0 errors) → `hf snapshot . --at <every beat> --no-end -o out/snaps`
>    → **look at every frame**: legible captions on light pages, nothing covering the eyes, no
>    blank captures, no placeholder → `FINAL=1 $EV1_PY build.py` → `hf render . -q high -f 30
>    -o out/render.mp4`.
> …
> 7. **Verify:** re-transcribe `out/final.mp4` and diff it against the take: a word the bed or a
>    hit masked shows up here. Then hand it over for review. Publish nothing.

## scripts/transcribe.py — how the words are found

`transcribe.py` runs the speech model in **20 s windows** and *"warns about dropped speech"*;
`snap-onsets.py` then puts *"the first word after each real pause"* on the sound *"not 60–220 ms
early"*. The worked example's timing is *"three 20 s windows stitched"*, from **parakeet**.
