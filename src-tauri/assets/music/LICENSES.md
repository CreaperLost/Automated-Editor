# Built-in music (SND-3)

Every track here is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free for monetised videos, changes and
redistribution, with no credit required. They are by two composers:

- **HoliznaCC0**, the account the composer Holizna keeps for public-domain releases (their bio
  on the Free Music Archive: "giving out truly unique and creative music for free as public
  domain"). Each track's own FMA page was checked for the CC0 licence link on 25 Oct 2026, as
  was each album's.
- **Komiku** (Rrrrrose Azerty's project; their FMA bio: "Use it how you want"). On 26 Oct 2026
  all 266 of their tracks on FMA, across 15 albums, linked CC0 1.0 on their own pages, with no
  other licence anywhere. No Komiku Bandcamp exists (komiku.bandcamp.com is unclaimed), so no
  second licence conflicts. Their moods below are from the titles and FMA genres: nobody has
  listened to them yet.

The app doesn't ship the files: `src-tauri/src/music_library.rs` lists each track's FMA file
with its size and SHA-256, and a track is downloaded the first time it is played or placed,
then kept in the app folder's `music/built-in`. A file that doesn't match its hash is refused.

## Not taken

- **Loyalty Freak Music.** archive.org lists its albums as CC0 and the artist's FAQ says
  "98 %" of their work is CC0, but the artist's own Bandcamp pages show **CC BY 4.0** for every
  album checked (Lofi Ambient Songs, Chill For Real, Minimal Ambient Bounce, To Chill And Stay
  Awake, Positive Attitude, Melodies With A Beat, Instrumental R&B Beats). Conflicting
  licences: refused.
- **archive.org's licence field in general.** It is set by whoever uploads, and is often wrong
  (game soundtracks and chart hits are marked CC0 there). A track is only taken when the
  artist's own page says CC0.
- **Holizna** (without "CC0"): the composer's other account, CC BY-ND and paid licences.
- **Pixabay and Mixkit music**: free without credit, but their licences forbid redistributing
  the tracks as a music library.
- **AI-generated "CC0" packs** (such as Suno-made collections on GitHub): whether the uploader
  holds any rights to waive is unclear.

Content ID: never register these tracks (or a video's music made from them) with a matching
service; that would claim other people's videos that use the same public-domain music.
