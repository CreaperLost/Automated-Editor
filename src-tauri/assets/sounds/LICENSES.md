# Built-in sound effects (SND-3)

Every sound here is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free for commercial use, changes and
redistribution, with no credit required. Each licence was checked on its source page on
25 Oct 2026. The files were trimmed, faded, resampled to 48 kHz and levelled (peak −3 dBFS,
whooshes and risers −6 dBFS), then saved as 16-bit FLAC.

Kenney's packs carry a `License.txt` saying: CC0, "free to use in personal, educational and
commercial projects", crediting Kenney "is not mandatory". Freesound pages show "Creative
Commons 0"; the Freesound files are the site's public HQ previews (MP3), since originals need
an account.
